Karjat Land Title Checks, Step by Step

The papers to ask for, the order to read them in, and the gaps one document can't cover.

Quick take

A 7/12 extract is a starting point, not proof of clean title. Ask for the mutation history, the NA order and a lawyer's 30-year search before paying anything.

Most land trouble around Karjat doesn't start with fraud. It starts with a buyer who saw one document and assumed it settled the question.

What the 7/12 Extract Shows

The 7/12 (Satbara utara) is the record of rights for a survey number. It names the holder, the area, the land use class and any rights recorded against it. Sellers usually hand it over first, and you can pull it from the Maharashtra land records portal to compare with the copy shown.

What it doesn't prove: the full chain of ownership, the absence of an unregistered agreement, or that an NA conversion is complete. This list of what to check when buying land in Karjat sets out the full document trail, and a buyer discussion on legal documents for plots in Maharashtra covers the questions people tend to forget.

The Papers to Ask For

DocumentWhat it tells you
7/12 extractCurrent holder, area, land class, recorded rights
Mutation entries (ferfar)How ownership changed hands over the years
8A extractAll holdings of the owner in that village
NA order or sanadThat non-agricultural use was permitted, and for what
Approved layout planPlot boundaries, roads and open spaces as sanctioned
Title search reportA lawyer's opinion on the ownership chain

"NA in Process" Is Not NA

Agricultural land in Maharashtra comes with purchase limits for non-agriculturists under Section 63 of the Tenancy Act. Until the NA order exists and the 7/12 reflects it, the land is still agricultural. Where MahaRERA Fits

A plotted layout larger than 500 sq. m., or with more than eight plots, generally has to be registered with MahaRERA before it is marketed. Registration puts the layout, the promoter's disclosures and the completion date on public record. It doesn't replace a title search, but it gives you something to check a sales pitch against. This plot legal and RERA guide explains how to read a registration page.

Common Slip-Ups

  • Checking the 7/12 but skipping the mutation history
  • Treating "NA in process" as equal to NA
  • Not walking the access road to see who owns it
  • Paying a token before a lawyer has seen the papers
  • Not checking whether the village falls in the eco-sensitive zone near Matheran

Quick Questions

Does a 7/12 extract prove clear title?

No. It shows the current recorded holder and rights. Clear title needs the mutation history, the NA order where relevant, and a lawyer's title search covering roughly 30 years.

Is an NA in process plot safe to buy?

It carries risk. Until the NA order is issued and shown on the 7/12, the land remains agricultural, with the purchase limits that apply to it.

Which plotted layouts need MahaRERA registration?

Layouts larger than 500 sq. m. or with more than eight plots generally need registration before they can be advertised or sold.
