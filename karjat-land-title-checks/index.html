Legal Basics

Karjat Land Title Checks: What the Paperwork Proves

The documents to ask for, the order to read them in, and the gaps a single extract can't cover.

Most land disputes around Karjat don't start with fraud. They start with a buyer who saw a document and assumed it settled the question. A 7/12 extract in hand is a beginning. It isn't a clean title.

What the 7/12 Extract Shows

The 7/12 (Satbara utara) is the record of rights for a survey number. It names the holder, the area, the land use class and any rights or encumbrances recorded against it. In the Karjat belt it is usually the first paper a seller hands over, and it can be pulled online from the Maharashtra land records portal to compare with the copy shown.

What it doesn't prove: a full chain of ownership, the absence of an unregistered agreement, or that an NA conversion is complete. Those need the mutation entries, the NA order itself and a 30-year search. This list of what to check when buying land in Karjat sets out the full document trail, and a buyer discussion on legal documents for plots in Maharashtra covers the questions people tend to forget.

Title search reportA lawyer's opinion on the ownership chain

NA Status Is Not a Formality

Agricultural land in Maharashtra comes with purchase limits for non-agriculturists under Section 63 of the Tenancy Act. Some sellers describe a plot as "NA in process". That phrase carries real risk. Until the order exists and the 7/12 reflects it, the plot is agricultural land. Where MahaRERA Fits In

A plotted layout larger than 500 sq. m., or one with more than eight plots, has to be registered with MahaRERA before it is marketed. Registration puts the layout, the promoter's disclosures and the completion date on public record. It doesn't replace a title search, but it gives buyers something to check the sales pitch against. Mistakes That Come Up Often

  • Checking the 7/12 but skipping the mutation history
  • Accepting "NA in process" as equivalent to NA
  • Not walking the access road to see who owns it
  • Paying a token before a lawyer has seen the papers
  • Ignoring whether the village falls in an eco-sensitive zone near Matheran

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a 7/12 extract prove clear title?

No. It shows the current recorded holder and rights. Clear title needs the mutation history, the NA order where relevant, and a lawyer's title search covering roughly 30 years.

Is an NA in process plot safe to buy?

It carries risk. Until the NA order is issued and reflected on the 7/12, the land remains agricultural, with the purchase limits that apply to it.

Which plotted layouts need MahaRERA registration?

Layouts larger than 500 sq. m. or with more than eight plots generally need registration before they can be advertised or sold.
