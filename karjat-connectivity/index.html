A plain status check on the roads, rail and airport that shape travel to Karjat.

Infrastructure is the reason most Mumbai buyers start looking at Karjat. It is also where most listings overstate things. Here is the status as of October 2026, separated into what already works and what is still being built.

The Panvel to Karjat rail link deserves a specific mention. Several listings describe it as operational. It isn't yet. Any travel-time claim built on it should be read as a future figure, not a current one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Panvel to Karjat local train running in 2026?

No. The Panvel to Karjat suburban rail link is still under construction. Karjat is served today by Central line trains from CSMT.

How long does it take to drive from Mumbai to Karjat?

Using Atal Setu, the drive takes roughly an hour in clear traffic. Peak-hour and monsoon travel can take longer.

Is Navi Mumbai International Airport open?

Yes. The airport is operational for commercial flights.
