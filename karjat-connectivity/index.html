Getting to Karjat: What Runs Today

A plain status check on the roads, rail and airport, separated into built and pending.

Quick take

Central line trains, Atal Setu and the new airport are all working. The Panvel to Karjat rail link is not running yet, whatever a listing says.

Infrastructure is the reason most Mumbai buyers start looking at Karjat. It is also where listings tend to overstate things. Here is the status as of October 2026.

Quick Questions

Is the Panvel to Karjat local train running in 2026?

No. It is still under construction. Karjat is served today by Central line trains from CSMT.

How long is the drive from Mumbai to Karjat?

Using Atal Setu, roughly an hour in clear traffic. Peak hours and monsoon days take longer.

Is Navi Mumbai International Airport open?

Yes. It is operational for commercial flights.
