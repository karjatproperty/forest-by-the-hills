Karjat Plot Prices: How Rates Are Set

The government floor, the seller's ask, and the extra costs that turn a headline price into the real one.

Quick take

Look up the Ready Reckoner rate first. Then compare plots like with like, and add stamp duty, registration and development charges before deciding.

Two plots a kilometre apart can be quoted at very different rates. That isn't always a sign one seller is wrong. Land class, road access, layout infrastructure and the view all move the number.

The Ready Reckoner Rate

The Ready Reckoner (Annual Statement of Rates) is set by the state registration department for each village and zone. Stamp duty is charged on the higher of this rate or the agreement value. It is a tax floor, not a market price. You can look it up on the IGR Maharashtra portal by district, taluka and village.

A breakdown of land prices by guntha, acre and sq. ft. explains the units sellers quote in. Agricultural land is often priced per guntha, while NA plots are usually priced per sq. ft.

Why Gated Plots Cost More per sq. ft.

A plot inside a gated layout carries the cost of internal roads, drainage, power lines, security and shared spaces. A standalone plot doesn't, but you then pay for those things separately or go without. The plot types comparison lays out the trade-off.

What Sits on Top of the Price

  • Stamp duty, on the higher of agreement value or Ready Reckoner value
  • Registration charges
  • Development or infrastructure charges in gated layouts
  • Maintenance deposits, where there are common areas
  • GST, where it applies to development components
  • Legal fees for the title search

This cost breakdown for an NA plot in Karjat works through these line by line, and a note on what influences NA plot costs covers the factors behind the rate.

Before You Negotiate

Pull the Ready Reckoner rate for the village. Ask whether the quoted price includes development charges. Confirm the plot area against the layout plan, not just a sales sheet. A buyer discussion comparing an NA plot with a suburban flat shows how different the cost structures can be. And do the title checks before any of this.

Quick Questions

What is the Ready Reckoner rate used for?

It is the government valuation used to calculate minimum stamp duty. Duty is charged on the higher of the Ready Reckoner value or the agreement value.

How many sq. ft. are in one guntha?

About 1,089 sq. ft., or roughly 101 sq. m. Forty guntha make one acre.

Why are gated plots priced higher per sq. ft.?

The rate includes internal roads, utilities, security and shared spaces. A standalone buyer pays for these separately or goes without.
