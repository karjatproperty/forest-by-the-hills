Where This Part of Karjat Sits

Karjat is a terminal stop on the Central line suburban network, so local trains from CSMT already run here. Road access improved sharply once Atal Setu opened in 2024. The drive from Mumbai now takes roughly an hour in clear traffic. Navi Mumbai International Airport is operational as well, which shortens travel for anyone flying in.

One correction to carry into any research: the Panvel to Karjat suburban rail link is still under construction. Some listings describe it as running. It isn't yet. The Karjat connectivity status page separates what is built from what is pending, and a newsletter tracking Mumbai property shifts follows the bigger regional picture week to week.

Land Types: Why NA Status Comes First

Much of the land around Karjat is still agricultural. That matters, because agricultural land in Maharashtra can't simply be bought by anyone and built on. Section 63 of the Maharashtra Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act restricts purchase by non-agriculturists, and conversion takes time.

NA (non-agricultural) plots skip that hurdle, since the conversion is already recorded. This breakdown of land types in Karjat explains each category, and a short note on Karjat land basics covers the local terms buyers run into during site visits.

Title Checks Before Any Token Amount

Every plot should carry its own 7/12 extract (Satbara utara) in the seller's name, with the NA order reflected on it. Where plots are sold within a layout, MahaRERA registration matters too. It puts the approved layout, the promoter's disclosures and the registered completion date on public record.

A step-by-step guide to Karjat land title checks goes through each document, and the Karjat plot legal and RERA guide shows how to read a registration properly. A lawyer's title search is still the one step that shouldn't be skipped.

How Plot Prices Work Here

Two numbers matter. The government Ready Reckoner rate sets the floor for stamp duty. The market rate is what sellers actually ask. Gated developments with internal roads, utilities and shared amenities price well above standalone plots, so a per sq. ft. comparison only works like for like.

A Short Pre-Purchase Checklist

  1. 7/12 extract in the seller's name, with the NA entry visible
  2. Copy of the NA order and the approved layout plan
  3. MahaRERA registration for layout plots, checked directly on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in
  4. Registered completion date versus the date quoted by the seller
  5. Access road status, public road or private easement
  6. Water source and power connection, existing or promised
  7. Ready Reckoner rate for the village against the asking price
  8. Independent title search by a property lawyer

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Forest by the Hills in Karjat?

It's associated with the Tata Power House Road side of Karjat, in the Sahyadri foothills. Confirm the exact village and survey numbers with the seller before a site visit.

Is the Panvel to Karjat rail link running?

Not yet. The corridor is still under construction. Karjat is currently served by Central line local trains from CSMT.

Can anyone buy agricultural land in Karjat?

Not directly. Section 63 of the Maharashtra Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act restricts purchase by non-agriculturists. NA plots, with the conversion already recorded on the 7/12 extract, avoid this restriction.

What should be checked before booking a plot?

The 7/12 extract, the NA order, the approved layout, MahaRERA registration where applicable, and an independent title search.
