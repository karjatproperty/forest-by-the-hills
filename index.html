Why So Many Mumbai Buyers Look Here

Karjat used to be a monsoon day trip. Waterfalls, a plate of vada pav at the station, back home by night. That has changed. With Atal Setu open and the new airport running, a lot of people now see it as a realistic place for a second home or a long-hold plot.

The pull is easy to understand. Matheran sits close by, the Ulhas river runs through the taluka, and the air is noticeably cleaner in winter. This local guide to Karjat covers villages, seasons and weekend routes, and a note on building a second home here looks at how people actually use their places. The wider shift east of Mumbai is tracked in a newsletter on Mumbai property trends.

A nice view doesn't make a safe purchase, though. Title, land type and approvals do. That's what the rest of this site is about.

How a Plot Purchase Usually Goes

  1. Shortlist the area. Visit on a weekday and again in the monsoon. Roads and drainage look very different in August.
  2. Check the land type. NA land can be built on. Agricultural land comes with purchase limits. This breakdown of land types in Karjat explains the categories.
  3. Read the papers. Start with the 7/12 extract, then the mutation history and the NA order. The title checks page walks through each one.
  4. Compare the price properly. Look up the Ready Reckoner rate, then compare like with like. See how plot prices are set.
  5. Check RERA for layouts. Larger plotted layouts must be registered. This plot legal and RERA guide shows how to read a registration.
  6. Get a lawyer's title search. Before any token amount. Every time.

Around Halivali

Halivali sits on the hill side of Karjat, close to the station. It is one of the pockets where planned plotted layouts have started to appear, mostly aimed at weekend buyers from Mumbai and Navi Mumbai.

One example is BluBay, a 60-acre gated layout of 325 NA plots with an individual 7/12 extract for each plot. It is registered with MahaRERA, with a registered completion date of December 2027, and the master plan follows the hill contours. As with any project, check the registration and papers yourself.

Quick Questions

Is Karjat a good place for a second home near Mumbai?

It suits buyers who want hills, greenery and a drive of about an hour from Mumbai. The practical test is whether the place will be used often enough, and whether the land papers are clean.

Is the Panvel to Karjat rail link running?

Not yet. The corridor is still under construction. Karjat is served today by Central line local trains from CSMT.

Can anyone buy agricultural land in Karjat?

Not directly. Section 63 of the Maharashtra Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act restricts purchase by non-agriculturists. NA plots, with the conversion recorded on the 7/12 extract, avoid this restriction.

What should be checked before booking a plot?

The 7/12 extract, the mutation history, the NA order, the approved layout, MahaRERA registration where it applies, and an independent title search.
